Club Leadership

The posts responsible for overall direction and coordination.

Top Leadership

Club President

Provides the overall direction of the club and ensures that its different functions work toward a common mission.

Responsibilities

  • Set the club's overall vision, priorities and strategic direction.
  • Coordinate the major committees and resolve cross-functional issues.
  • Make or oversee major organizational decisions.
  • Represent the club before the college and external stakeholders.
  • Build a strong leadership culture and ensure continuity of the organization.
Must be really good atSystems thinking, judgment, leadership and making high-quality decisions under ambiguity.
Executive Leadership

Vice President

Supports the President and coordinates the functioning of the major committees.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate committee-level execution.
  • Track progress across major functions.
  • Support the President in implementing strategy.
  • Resolve operational dependencies between committees.
  • Step in to maintain continuity when required.
Must be really good atCoordination, prioritization, communication and turning strategy into execution.

Organising Committee

Research, operations, relationships and resources that keep the organization moving.

Organising

Research Lead

Owns research activities that support the club's problem-solving work and decisions.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate research activities and evidence collection.
  • Support problem investigation and validation.
  • Organize findings, references and research outputs.
  • Help teams distinguish assumptions from evidence.
Must be really good atResearch methodology, critical thinking and extracting useful evidence.
Organising

Operations Lead

Ensures that the club's internal activities, processes and administration run reliably.

Responsibilities

  • Coordinate meetings, schedules and internal processes.
  • Maintain records and documentation.
  • Support recruitment and assessment logistics.
  • Coordinate administrative requirements and execution.
Must be really good atOrganization, reliability, follow-through and process management.
Organising

Partnerships Lead

Builds and manages relationships with people and organizations outside the club.

Responsibilities

  • Develop relationships with clubs, communities, alumni and experts.
  • Coordinate mentors, speakers and external collaborators.
  • Identify external opportunities for projects and members.
  • Support sponsorship and institutional partnerships.
Must be really good atCommunication, networking, negotiation and relationship building.
Organising

Treasury & Logistics Lead

Manages the club's money, procurement, physical resources and logistical execution.

Responsibilities

  • Prepare and monitor budgets.
  • Track income, expenditure and reimbursements.
  • Coordinate procurement and vendors.
  • Manage equipment, inventory and resource allocation.
  • Coordinate venues, materials and event logistics.
Must be really good atFinancial discipline, planning, resource management and operational precision.

Tech Committee

Builds and maintains the club's digital products, systems and technical capabilities.

Technology

Product & Systems Lead

Turns organizational needs into clearly defined digital products and systems.

Responsibilities

  • Gather requirements and understand user needs.
  • Define product specifications and workflows.
  • Prioritize features and maintain product roadmaps.
  • Coordinate UX and usability testing.
  • Bridge users, leadership and engineering.
Must be really good atProduct thinking, problem decomposition and translating needs into systems.
Technology

Frontend Lead

Owns the user-facing interfaces of the club's digital systems.

Responsibilities

  • Build responsive web or application interfaces.
  • Establish frontend standards and reusable components.
  • Integrate interfaces with APIs.
  • Test usability and frontend reliability.
  • Review and guide frontend contributions.
Must be really good atFrontend engineering, UI implementation and attention to user experience.
Technology

Backend Lead

Owns server-side systems, APIs, databases and application logic.

Responsibilities

  • Design backend architecture and APIs.
  • Develop application services and business logic.
  • Design and manage databases.
  • Implement authentication and authorization.
  • Maintain reliability, scalability and security.
Must be really good atBackend architecture, databases, APIs and reliable system design.
Technology

Data Lead

Owns the collection, management, analysis and use of organizational data.

Responsibilities

  • Design data structures and pipelines.
  • Maintain data quality and documentation.
  • Build dashboards and analytics.
  • Analyze member, project and organizational data.
  • Support evidence-based decision-making.
Must be really good atData thinking, analytical reasoning and turning data into useful decisions.
Technology

AI Lead

Owns the development and responsible use of AI systems across the club.

Responsibilities

  • Identify useful AI opportunities.
  • Develop AI/ML and AI-powered features.
  • Work with models, LLMs, agents and automation.
  • Evaluate model performance and reliability.
  • Guide AI experimentation and responsible use.
Must be really good atAI/ML fundamentals, experimentation and judging where AI genuinely adds value.

Marketing Committee

Builds the club's identity, communicates its work and grows participation.

Marketing

Brand & Creativity Lead

Owns the club's visual identity and creative direction.

Responsibilities

  • Maintain brand guidelines and visual consistency.
  • Create or direct posters, graphics and presentation assets.
  • Guide creative campaigns and visual storytelling.
  • Coordinate designers and creative contributors.
Must be really good atVisual communication, creativity and maintaining a coherent brand.
Marketing

Content & Media Lead

Owns what the club communicates and how its work is documented.

Responsibilities

  • Plan content and editorial calendars.
  • Create social media content and announcements.
  • Document projects, members and events.
  • Coordinate articles, interviews, photography and video.
  • Maintain the quality and consistency of public communication.
Must be really good atStorytelling, writing, media production and understanding an audience.
Marketing

Growth & Outreach Lead

Owns the growth of the club's community, participation and reach.

Responsibilities

  • Plan student recruitment and outreach campaigns.
  • Mobilize volunteers and project participants.
  • Reach departments, student groups and campus communities.
  • Promote problem submissions and project opportunities.
  • Track recruitment and engagement performance.
Must be really good atPersuasion, outreach strategy, networking and understanding what motivates participation.

Associate Layer

The distributed execution layer beneath the functional Leads.

Tech Associates

Execute defined technical work such as features, testing, data tasks, documentation, automation and system support under the relevant Tech Lead.

Low authority · High execution
Marketing Associates

Execute distributed marketing work such as designs, content, social media, event coverage, outreach and campaign support under the relevant Marketing Lead.

Low authority · High execution

How the structure works

Authority should decrease downward while execution capacity increases.
